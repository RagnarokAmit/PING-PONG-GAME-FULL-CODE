HOW TO PLAY

Instructions

  1. Keep your laptop screen straight and position the camera so that your upper body is clearly visible.
  2. Move approximately 3–4 feet away from your laptop.
  3. Move your right wrist up and down.
  4. Once PoseNet detects your wrist, a red marker will appear on it.
  5. Press Play Game to start.
  6. Your red paddle follows the vertical movement of your right wrist.
  7. The computer controls the orange paddle.
